Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about becoming an Etsy shop partner.

Do I need to manage or operate the Etsy store myself?

No, absolutely not. Your role is solely assisting with the shop application and identity verification. Once verified, our professional international operations team handles product listing, customer service, shipping, and order management.

How long does the shop setup process take?

The entire process requires roughly 1 hour of your time to coordinate with our agent and perform verification.

Is there any financial risk or upfront fee required from me?

Zero cost and zero financial risk. You do not need to invest any money, purchase inventory, or pay registration fees. We cover all operational costs.

How will I receive my ₱5,000–₱10,000 payout?

Payouts are issued directly to your preferred Philippine e-wallet (such as GCash or Maya) or local bank account as soon as the store verification passes successfully.

How does the monthly store rent and profit sharing work?

After the store goes live and active, we pay you a fixed monthly rental fee for store usage. For reliable long-term partners, you will also receive a percentage share of the store's monthly profit dividends.

What products are sold in the store? Are they legal?

We sell 100% legal everyday consumer products including home décor, beauty supplies, lifestyle accessories, and daily necessities. All products fully comply with local and international trade laws.
